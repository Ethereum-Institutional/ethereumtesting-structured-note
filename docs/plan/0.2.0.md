# Plan for 0.2.0

Written 14 October 2026, after the working group's call of 13 October, and kept as the record of
what 0.2.0 delivered. 0.2.0 is what the bank's IT team received: the code on GitHub, and a PDF of
the solution overview. Release 0.3.0 (`docs/plan/0.3.0.md`) turned its design into a note
programme; the decision records describe the design as it stands now.

## What the call settled

- **The bank's side runs on a standalone virtual machine,** built and run by the bank's IT team,
  partly through an AI-led development process, from our requirements, our PDF and our code.
- **The note moves from ERC-7943 to ERC-3643,** to align with a parallel proof of concept at the
  bank.
- **One master template and a factory.** Shared scaffolding (identity, onboarding,
  administration), and a factory that issues a token for each deal.
- **The settlement asset is a tokenized deposit,** an allowlist token. No stablecoin and no
  cryptocurrency touch the transaction.
- **The success criteria are the bank's nine:** full deal lifecycle, issuance, a coupon, a
  secondary transfer, KYC whitelist enforcement, registry reconciliation, day-to-day
  reconciliation, issuer buy-back, and logic validation.
- **The bank would send** its list of term-sheet fields and its detailed order of lifecycle events.

## Decisions taken (owner, 14 October 2026)

1. **ERC-3643 through T-REX,** the reference implementation, pinned at 5.0.0-beta2 with ONCHAINID.
   T-REX is GPL-3.0 and this repository MPL-2.0; the boundary is kept narrow and stated for the
   bank's legal review.
2. **One identity per client, with claims by role.** The bank vouches for its deposit clients; an
   onboarding agent vouches for the note. The Agent sees wallets, identities and who vouched, never
   personal data. Whoever holds the wallet's key manages the identity: the bank's custody platform,
   a third-party custodian, or the client.
3. **The conversion pool,** first planned as an optional appendix, is deployed by default and gated
   on the same identities. Settlement is the tokenized deposit alone.
4. **Everything moves to Ethereum Sepolia.** Two complete deployments on the same code, ours and the
   bank's, each with its own keys and records; neither depends on the other. Tests stay on anvil.
5. **The external credential platform is removed entirely.** Onboarding rests on ERC-3643's own
   identities and claims: claims that lapse by themselves, wallet control by a typed signature, and
   an existing identity brought to the note in place of the holder-wallet route.

## What 0.2.0 delivered

| # | Workstream | Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Research | T-REX 5.0.0-beta2 with each agent's powers restricted; the deposit as an allowlist ERC-20 eligible by the bank's own identity registry; a narrow GPL boundary; Safe, Permit2, EURC and Uniswap v4 confirmed at their canonical addresses on Ethereum Sepolia |
| 1 | ERC-3643 core | The note as a T-REX token on ONCHAINID identities, with its own fixed implementation authority in place of T-REX's switchable one |
| 2 | Onboarding by role | Five portals; claim issuers for the bank and the onboarding agent, with expiry; four routes: bank custody, third-party custodian, self-custody, and an existing identity |
| 3 | The factory | A note issued whole in one transaction, in place of the site's nineteen |
| 4 | The lifecycle on ERC-3643 | Record dates as a compliance module; the Paying Agent and settlement as restricted agents; wallet recovery; the governance controller as owner |
| 5 | The tokenized deposit | Credit, debit, hold, pause and a move by order with its reason, on the bank's Deposit page |
| 6 | The pool | On by default, its gate reading the same identities |
| 7 | Day-to-day reconciliation | A statement for every note as at the close of any day, now or a block, in JSON and CSV |
| 8 | The term sheet as data | A typed record, every field present or "not applicable", the input to issuance, its hash on the note |
| 9 | The order of lifecycle events | `docs/lifecycle-sequence.md`, walked step by step by `instruments/test/Sequence.t.sol` |
| 10 | Deployment | Ethereum Sepolia on 15 October 2026; the site's container bundle in `ops/vm/`; the IT hand-over guide and the AI build brief |
| 11 | Documents | The overview rewritten, with its PDF generated from it |
| 12 | Release | Tests on anvil and a tagged release, `v0.2.0` |

## Left open at 0.2.0

- From the bank: the term-sheet field list, and the detailed order of lifecycle events. Both are
  still awaited.
- From the bank's markets team: who plays the onboarding agent, and whether it must be licensed.
- **Risks named then,** still standing: T-REX's licence beside ours; a pre-release T-REX, pinned
  exactly with every restriction tested; small, unaudited community code for the deposit's
  freezing, vendored at a pinned commit and fully tested.
