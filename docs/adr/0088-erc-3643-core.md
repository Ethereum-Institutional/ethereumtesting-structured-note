# 0088. The note as an ERC-3643 token: identities, claims by role, record dates as compliance, and restricted agents

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 14 October 2026), as workstream 1 of 0.2.0
- **Date:** 14 October 2026

## Context

0.2.0 makes the note an ERC-3643 token through T-REX (plan, decision 1), with one identity per
client and claims by role (decision 2). ADRs 0085 to 0087 set the release, the deposit and the
licences. This one records how the contracts are built.

Building on T-REX 5.0.0-beta2 settled several further questions, and turned up one defect:
- **A defect in T-REX's implementation authority.** In 5.0.0-beta2, `addAndUseTREXVersion` calls the internal add and use functions without the owner check that 4.1.3 and 4.2.0 have. Anyone could therefore add a version and switch the logic behind every proxy that reads the authority.
- **Claim issuers cannot add claims as ONCHAINID 2.2.2-beta3 is written.** ONCHAINID's `execute` lets a claim key add a claim, but `approve` then requires a management key. So `addClaimTo` works only for an issuer that manages the client's identity.
- **Recovery moves units without calling compliance.** T-REX's `recoveryAddress` does so, and forced transfers skip the compliance check.
- **The shared identity storage must be owned by T-REX's factory.** A note's suite can bind to it only then, and the factory cannot accept ownership later.

## Decision

### The scaffolding: once per deployment (`script/Scaffolding.sol`)

**ONCHAINID:**
- ONCHAINID's identity factory, handed to the bank, which creates every identity.
- The identity logic is fixed for good: its implementation authority's owner renounces.

**T-REX:**
- One implementation of each T-REX contract, behind `FixedImplementationAuthority`, our own small contract.
  - It answers the six questions a proxy or T-REX's factory asks it, and nothing else.
  - It cannot add a version, switch one, or move a proxy. That closes the defect above, and the note's logic stays the logic it was issued with.
- T-REX's factory, owned by the Issuer to issue notes.

**Claims and the deposit:**
- **Two claim issuers,** each an `ExpiringClaimIssuer` managed by its institution's key: the bank's and the onboarding agent's.
- **One identity storage,** owned by T-REX's factory, shared by every registry.
- **The deposit's own identity registry,** with its claim topics and trusted issuers. It trusts the bank's issuer for the deposit claim, and the bank owns and administers it.
- **The deposit** (ADR 0086) and its gateway.

### Claims (`src/claims`)

**Topics:**

| Topic | What it says | Issued by |
|---|---|---|
| `claim.deposit-eligible` | The client may hold the deposit | The bank |
| `claim.note-eligible` | The client is eligible for the note under its selling restrictions, and sanctions-screened | An onboarding agent |
| `claim.term-sheet` | The term sheet's hash and location | The note itself, on its own identity |

**A claim's data** is `abi.encode(issuedAt, validUntil, recordHash)`. The record hash is a salted hash of the verified record the institution keeps off-chain, so nothing on-chain identifies the client (Q1).

**`ExpiringClaimIssuer`** (Q3) treats a claim as valid while all of these hold:
- one of its claim keys signed it;
- it is not revoked;
- it was issued no later than now;
- it has not yet lapsed;
- it runs no longer than the issuer's `maxValidity`.

So a lapsed claim needs no transaction to take effect.

**Onboarding** (`script/Onboarding.sol`) has three steps:
1. The bank creates the client's identity, manages it, and registers the wallet against it.
2. The client's consent is recorded as a claim key on its identity for the institution's own key.
3. The institution signs its claim and adds it to the identity itself, naming its claim issuer. ONCHAINID checks the claim against that issuer before accepting it.

### Each note (`script/Issuance.sol`)

**The suite:**
- **One T-REX suite** through T-REX's factory, on the shared identity storage.
- **Decimals zero,** so a balance is a count of €1,000 units.
- **The note's registry** requires `claim.note-eligible`, from the onboarding agent's issuer. The onboarding agent is its agent.
- **The note's compliance** carries one module: the note's `RecordDateModule`.
- **The term sheet** is anchored as a self-attested claim on the note's own identity.

**The record-date module** takes the place of the snapshot engine, with the same rules:
- **E1.** Every mint, burn and transfer is checkpointed at its block time.
- **E4, ordinary transfers.** From a redemption Record Time until it is determined, an ordinary transfer is refused by the compliance check before it moves.
- **E4, forced transfers.** These skip the check, so the module refuses them after the move, which undoes them.
- **Recovery.** It moves units without calling compliance, so `sync` records the accounts' balances afterwards.
- **Binding.** One module per note: it binds to the first compliance that binds it. It is not upgradeable.

**Agents,** each limited by `setAgentRestrictions` (ADR 0085):

| Agent | May | Until |
|---|---|---|
| The Issuer | mint, pause and unpause | the ceremony |
| The Paying Agent | burn, pause | for good |
| The settlement contract | freeze and unfreeze part of a holding | for good |
| The governance controller | mint, burn, forced transfer, freeze an address, recover a wallet | from the ceremony |
| The servicing wallet | pause, freeze part of a holding | from the ceremony |

### The ceremony and the controller

**The ceremony** (`script/Governance.sol`):
- The controller becomes owner of the note and of its identity registry, trusted issuers, claim topics and compliance. Each is offered and accepted in the same sequence, through `acceptOwnerships`.
- The controller becomes the manager of the note's identity.
- The Issuer stops being an agent.
- The deployer renounces its admin roles.
- The Issuer keeps T-REX's factory, to issue further notes.

**The controller's acts, mapped onto ERC-3643:**

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Tier 1: correct the register by forced transfer, with its reason | The same, through T-REX's forced transfer; the reason is emitted by the controller |
| Tier 1: withdraw any grant (supervisory) | Tier 1: freeze or unfreeze an account at once, whichever institution vouched for it |
| (none) | Tier 1: recover a holder's units from a lost wallet to a new one (S-25), never while transfers are stopped |
| Tier 2: set a registrar, set the maximum validity | Tier 2: trust a claim issuer for the note's topics, change its topics, or cease to trust it. Each institution sets its own issuer's maximum validity |
| Tier 2: upgrade a token, replace a gate | Removed: nothing is upgradeable, and the compliance is fixed at issuance |
| Accept the venue's ownership | `acceptOwnerships`, for the note's suite and the venue alike |

### Licences (ADR 0087)

- **GPL-3.0:**
  - the record-date module and the claim issuer;
  - the deployment libraries and tests, which compile T-REX in.
- **MPL-2.0:**
  - the Paying Agent, the settlement contract, the governance controller, the Determination Agent, the deposit, the gateway and the fixed implementation authority;
  - all of these reach T-REX through `src/interfaces/INote.sol`.

## Consequences

- **Retired:**
  - the debt standard (submodule and pin check);
  - our Identity Registry, grant flags and Instrument Gate;
  - the snapshot engine and the old delivery-versus-payment contract;
  - the conformance tests that went with them.
- **The instruments compile with solc 0.8.30,** with dependencies pinned in `instruments/soldeer.lock`.
- **Tests:** 80 Foundry tests across lifecycle, settlement, instruments and identities, governance, and all 25 payoff scenarios. Each scenario is issued as a note of its own on the same scaffolding to the same clients, so the identities are shown serving every note. The four fork tests pass through the real Safe wallets on a local chain.
- **Not yet ported** (later workstreams of the plan):
  - the venue, custody and attestation scripts at the repository root, which still write to the retired registry;
  - the site's onboarding, issuance and governance pages.
  - Until then, `ops/reset.sh` does not deploy the whole stack on this branch.
- **The defect in T-REX 5.0.0-beta2** is avoided here, not fixed. It should be reported to the T-REX Network privately before 0.2.0 is shared.
